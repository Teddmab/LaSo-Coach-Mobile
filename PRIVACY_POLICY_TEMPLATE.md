# Privacy Policy for LasoCoach

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Email address**: For account creation and communication
- **Name**: For personalization of the app experience
- **Profile information**: Height, weight, fitness goals (optional)
- **Health data**: Workout progress, nutrition tracking (stored locally and on our servers)

- **App usage**: Features used, time spent in app
- **Device information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Analytics data**: Crash reports, performance metrics

- **Subscription status**: Active/inactive subscription status

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Service delivery**: Provide nutrition plans, workout tracking, progress monitoring
- **Account management**: Maintain your account and preferences
- **Communication**: Send important updates about your subscription and app features
- **Personalization**: Customize content based on your goals and preferences

### Analytics and Improvement
- **App improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- **Crash reporting**: Fix bugs and improve stability
- **Performance monitoring**: Ensure optimal app performance

## Data Storage and Security

### Where We Store Data
- **Cloud servers**: Your profile and progress data stored securely on encrypted servers
- **Local storage**: Some data cached on your device for offline access
- **Third-party services**: Analytics and crash reporting (anonymized)

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted using HTTPS encryption
- **Access controls**: Limited access to personal data by authorized personnel only
- **Regular audits**: Security practices reviewed regularly

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Store
- **Payment processing**: Subscription payments processed by Google
- **Receipt validation**: We validate purchase receipts with Google's servers
- **No payment data stored**: We never store your payment information

### Analytics Services
- **Crash reporting**: Anonymous crash data to improve app stability
- **Usage analytics**: Aggregated usage statistics (no personal identification)
